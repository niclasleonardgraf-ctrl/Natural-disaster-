## Textures (all original, CC0)
Generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`: soft 64x64 fractal-noise cloud, cloud-base, funnel-streak, dust and rain-curtain
textures, 8x8 debris specks, mod logo, and the replacement `minecraft:textures/environment/rain.png` (realistic rain).
Wind-storm particles use the same procedural approach: soft motion-blur streaks for blizzard snow and sand grains, and
128x128 billowy haze puffs for snow and dust. The sand layer blocks reuse vanilla's sand / red sand textures and
snow-layer model shapes (referenced, not copied).
